Boxes are cheap again. Nobody told the shelves.

Spot rates on the Asia–Europe lane have halved since spring, yet retailers report no change at the till.

By Ines Marchetti, Freight Editor · 6 min read

The cranes at Rotterdam have not been this idle since the spring of 2019. Along Quay 14, stacks of empty forty-footers wait for a cargo that is slow to arrive. Shipowners, who made a fortune from scarcity, now discuss blank sailings with the grim politeness of men at a funeral. The ledger of the past year tells a plainer story: the price of moving a box fell, and the price of what is inside it did not.
